Welcome, {{ request.user.get_full_name|default:request.user.username }}

This is your LIB POS App dashboard. Below is an overview of what you can do in the system depending on your role.

👑 Super Admin Capabilities

  • 📦 Product Management: Create, update, and delete products with details like name, price, and description.
  • 🧾 Sales Processing: Add products to customer orders, set quantities, apply tax, and confirm transactions.
  • 📤 Send Receipts: Automatically generate and email PDF-like receipts to customers after every sale.
  • 📉 Low Stock Warning: Get alerts for products running low on stock.
  • 📜 Sales History: View all sales made, including customer details, product quantities, and timestamps.
  • 👥 Admin Management: Add, update, or remove admin users in the system (stored in the `auth_user` table).
  • 📊 Dashboard Analytics: Get a quick overview of recent sales and system activity.

🔐 Regular Admin/User Capabilities

  • 🛒 Make Sales: Process new customer orders by selecting products and entering quantities.
  • 🧮 Auto Billing: System calculates total amount, applies tax, and generates the final bill.
  • 📬 Send Receipts: Email a professional receipt to the customer's email after each transaction.
  • 📄 View Sales History: See a list of sales you’ve processed and access details for each.

🧭 How to Use the System

  1. Login: Use your assigned credentials to log in.
  2. Navigate: Use the sidebar to access Products, Sales, Admins, and Sales History.
  3. Manage Products: Add or edit products to keep inventory up to date.
  4. Make a Sale: Go to the "New Sale" section, select products and quantities, and complete the sale.
  5. Send Receipt: After sale confirmation, email the receipt directly from the sales detail page.
  6. Track Sales: Use the Sales History page to view past sales, including details and receipt links.
  7. Super Admin Only: Manage other admin users from the “Admin” section in the sidebar.

🌍 System Time Zone

The system is currently set to Africa/Kigali time for accurate transaction timestamps.
